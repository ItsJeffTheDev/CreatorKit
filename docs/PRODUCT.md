# CreatorKit — Product Notes

## Purpose
A practical, low-friction workspace for creators who want to plan content without juggling several spreadsheets and notes apps.

## Product principles
1. Start empty: never seed fake ideas, videos, analytics, or subscribers.
2. Be honest: manually entered statistics are labeled as user-provided; do not imply a YouTube API connection where none exists.
3. Keep creators in control: support backup and restore.
4. Work on small screens as well as desktop.
5. Do not ship fake checkout flows. Billing must be verified by a trusted backend.

## Current workspace areas
- Dashboard: summaries derived from the user's own saved records
- Idea vault: searchable ideas with categories and statuses
- Content calendar: target dates and workflow stages
- Script builder: editable writing structures
- Upload checklist: per-video readiness tasks
- Creator progress: goals and manually entered stats
- Settings & backup: workspace settings and exports

## Not in scope until implemented and tested
- Automatic YouTube analytics
- Guaranteed growth predictions or viral-score claims
- Real recurring billing unless a production payment backend is configured and tested
- Multi-device cloud sync outside the authenticated AppDeploy workspace

## Success criteria
- New users see an empty, understandable workspace
- Core records can be created, edited, searched, and deleted
- Backup restore is validated before replacing current data
- Layout works on mobile and desktop
- No misleading claims about integrations, analytics, or payments
